# Brag Plan: FixLanka

## What is this app?
A bilingual mobile-first directory for Sri Lanka — find a trusted plumber, electrician, or AC tech near you and call them directly in one tap, no middleman.

## The angle
Sri Lanka has 22 million people and zero good ways to find a local technician. FixLanka fixes that. The video plays it straight: real app, real flow, real contact — warm and community-first, not corporate.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
White bold text slams onto the dark navy hero background:
> "Your AC broke at midnight."
Then a beat of silence. Then:
> "One tap. Fixed."

The dot-grid and blue glow breathe in behind it. No transition — just presence.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The category grid pops in chip by chip — Plumbing (blue), Electrical (yellow), AC (sky), Appliances (violet) — each with its colored icon and a card-place sound
- A technician card slides up: gradient avatar "KP", Kamal Perera — Colombo, Plumbing, "From LKR 2,500" — with a WhatsApp button glowing green
- Simulated WhatsApp tap — green button pulses, screen transition implies the call is happening

## Outro / punchline
Dark navy screen, white text:
> "Find trusted technicians near you."
> **FixLanka**
Small subline: "Free. Direct. Local."

## User flow worth showing
1. Hero — dark screen with bold headline hook
2. Category chips pop in one by one (Plumbing selected, glows blue)
3. Technician card appears — real data, avatar, price, WhatsApp button
4. Simulated tap on WhatsApp → implies connection

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: warm community app launch — local, real, no puff
- Interpretation: comfortable pace with room for each moment; warm music bed; SFX reward each card arrival; restraint on transitions

## Format: vertical — 1080×1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: warm upbeat bed with tasteful card-arrival SFX
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM, mid-energy)
- Music treatment: fade in from 0s at vol 0.32; duck to 0.12 during voiceover; fade out last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset available. Strong cues at 4.23s (hook reveal), 6.34s (category grid start), 10.54s (technician card reveal), 12.65s (WhatsApp tap moment). Beat grid at ~0.52s intervals for chip sequential reveals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; hero dot-grid glow and card presence breathe gently with music RMS — no waveform visuals
- SFX posture: moderate; motion-matched; professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Hook text slam → `impactSoft_medium_001.ogg` at 0.05s
  - Each category chip arrival → `drop_001.ogg` / `drop_002.ogg` alternating, beat-grid aligned (~6.34–7.92s)
  - Technician card slide-up → `card-place-1.ogg` at ~10.40s (near strong cue 10.54s)
  - WhatsApp tap pulse → `bong_001.ogg` at ~14.22s
  - Logo/outro slam → `impactBell_heavy_000.ogg` at ~17.38s
- Restraint rule: music must not overpower voiceover; SFX must not stack on every beat
